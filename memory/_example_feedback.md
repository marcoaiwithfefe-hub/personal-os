Alex prefers recipes written in grams and milliliters, not cups and ounces.

**Why:** Alex's kitchen scale and all supplier packaging are already metric. Converting from imperial mid-bake wastes time and introduces rounding errors on ratios that matter, i.e. hydration, butter fat.

**How to apply:** When writing or updating a bakery recipe in `Knowledge/bakery/`, use grams and milliliters by default. If an existing note is still in imperial, convert it when you next touch it rather than leaving mixed units in the same note.
